Our story · since 1927

An osteria, the way it used to be.

In 1927, in the old town of Sant'Agata de' Goti, the Antica Osteria di Zi' Pauluccio was born. Ever since, it has been a place of hospitality, local cooking and conviviality.

Identity

A family name, a table for everyone.

"Zi'", in Campanian dialect, means uncle: the family figure who opens the door and finds everyone a seat. It's how guests describe the osteria — rustic and well-kept, family-style service, simple dishes done well.

Stone, wood, warm light, a deep cellar below the dining room: the osteria keeps the atmosphere of the old town's houses.

01

Setting

An intimate dining room on Via Roma with a few well-placed tables. Guests appreciate the attention to detail.

02

Philosophy

Sannio cooking, local ingredients and traditional recipes, without artifice.

03

Hospitality

Attentive, warm service — the compliment that recurs most in reviews.
