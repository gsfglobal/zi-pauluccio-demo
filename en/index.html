02 · The soul of the osteria

An osteria of stone and wood, on Via Roma.

Since 1927, on the old town's main street, the Antica Osteria has welcomed guests in an intimate, carefully kept dining room. A place where time slows and the table becomes a ritual again.

  • 01

    Hospitality

    Guests describe attentive, family-style service — like dining at a friend's home.

  • 02

    Tradition

    Recipes of the Sannio territory, cooked simply with local produce.

  • 03

    The cellar

    Below the dining room, a deep cellar that guests recommend visiting.

Our story

03 · The taste of tradition

Recipes that taste of home and land.

Honest cooking rooted in Sannio produce and family recipes. The dishes here are the ones guests mention most.

  • Pancotto

    Bread, greens and oil: humble cooking turned memory.

  • Fresh pasta

    Ravioli, cavatelli, tortelli — dressed with slow sauces.

  • Caciocavallo

    Cave-aged, baked, with roasted tomatoes and bruschetta for mopping up.

  • The grill

    Slow-cooked artichoke, sliced steak, Castelpoto sausage.

06 · A table in the heart of the old town

Sant'Agata de' Goti, pearl of the Sannio.

Houses perched on the cliff, bell towers and majolica domes, stone alleys. Lunch or dinner here is part of the journey.

  • Tufa

    The town rises on a tufa spur overlooking the Isclero valley.

  • Orange Flag

    Awarded by the Touring Club Italiano since 2004.

  • Via Roma

    About a kilometre of history — and at number 27, your table.

Sources: Touring Club Italiano, FAI · Panoramic photo to be shot

07 · Our cellar

Beneath our feet, the wine.

Guests describe it as a remarkably deep cellar, worth a visit. This is Aglianico and Falanghina country, with its own Sant'Agata dei Goti DOC: the right glass for every dish.
